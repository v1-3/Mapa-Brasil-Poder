The index, year by year

Each strand is a party line, and its thickness is that line’s Party Political Power Index that year. Strands keep a fixed order from left (top) to right (bottom), so they never cross. Hover or tap a year, or use the arrow keys. The dashed line marks where election results replace estimated Senate seats; lighter strands are partly estimated years.
