PPI (institutional weights, median of 5,000 weight and seat draws) is scored for the six term starts 2003–2023; earlier periods and unmeasured small parties use a fixed thin width. The faint halo marks the 90th percentile. The time axis is compressed in four segments (1822–1889, 1889–1945, 1945–1985, 1985–2026). “In government” = parties voting with the government at least 60% of the time (2003 onward; the main governing parties before that, approximate). Leaders are heads of state; portraits can be added in PHOTOS.
